Made for Bengaluru

A generic spec does not survive this city

Four conditions decide how a Bengaluru home ages. Every material we specify is chosen against them, and we say which one it is answering.

Dust

Metro work, road cutting, four dry months

Matt and suede finishes hold fingerprints and grit far worse than a fine gloss or a textured laminate. We keep open shelving low and handleless profiles out of the kitchen.

Moisture

Two monsoons and a damp north wall

710-grade BWP ply for anything near water or an external wall: ₹95-130 per sq ft against ₹65 for MR. That difference is the whole argument, and it is worth it in exactly three rooms.

Water

Borewell hardness on every fitting

Hard water scars chrome and etches dark granite within two years. Brushed finishes, quartz counters and a softener point costed into the plan rather than added later.

Daily life

Compact flats, long commutes, help twice a week

Storage sized to what you own, surfaces that wipe down in one pass, and no detail that needs monthly attention from someone who is not home.

The problem

Why ₹8 lakh becomes ₹14 lakh

Three things go wrong, and they go wrong in the same order almost every time.

01

The quote is a number, not a scope

One line reading "Modular kitchen, ₹3,50,000" hides forty decisions: carcass grade, shutter finish, hardware brand, counter thickness. Every one of them is a place the price can move, and it only moves up.

02

Costs arrive late, one at a time

False ceiling, electrical rework, loose furniture, curtains, GST. Each one sounds small on a Tuesday phone call. Together they land 40 to 70% above the number you signed.

03

Your designer is also your vendor

When the person specifying the plywood also sells the plywood, there is no version of the quote you can check. The design looks free because it is buried in the material rate.

FAQ

The questions people ask first

Do you charge a consultation fee?

The first 30 minutes are free, on call or at your site. If you go ahead, a full 2BHK plan is a flat ₹45,000, a single room or kitchen-only costing starts at ₹12,000, and larger projects are 4 to 6% of value. You see the number before we start, and it does not change later.

Do you take vendor commissions?

No. Not from carpenters, not from modular brands, not from material suppliers. It is written into our engagement letter as a term you can hold us to. Partners are on our list because their work stood up on site, and if one of them offers a cut we tell you who and how much.

What if my budget is under ₹5L?

Then we plan for under ₹5L. A kitchen, two wardrobes, paint and lighting is a real project and it deserves a real drawing set. We scope it, cost it at ₹12,000 to ₹20,000, and match you to partners who work at that size without cutting the ply grade behind your back.

Do you execute the work yourselves?

No. You contract partners directly, so you hold the money and the leverage. We review their quotes against our costing, sit in on the negotiation, and inspect the site at four stages: after demolition, after carpentry framing, before painting, and at handover.

Why does a Bengaluru-specific plan matter?

Because the same catalogue kitchen behaves differently on Sarjapur Road than it does in a dry city. Road dust settles into matt shutters, monsoon damp travels through an un-battened north wall, and borewell water pits a chrome tap within two years. Specifying against those three things costs nothing extra at the drawing stage and saves rework later.
